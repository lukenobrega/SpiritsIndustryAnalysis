# Spirits Industry Market Analysis

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Data Analysis | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence**

An independent data analytics project completed during my internship at **Disaronno International USA**, examining historical and projected trends in the U.S. and global spirits industry.

The project involved processing large Excel datasets, analyzing sales volume and value across spirits categories and geographic markets, and communicating the findings through an executive presentation. The analysis identified industry trends related to premiumization, market growth, and product category performance.

Findings from the presentation were subsequently shared with the CEO of Disaronno International USA and the CEO of its parent company, Disaronno Group, and contributed to a LinkedIn post discussing premiumization and whiskey market trends.

---

## Project Background

During my internship at Disaronno International USA, I independently approached the Finance and Analytics team seeking opportunities to apply programming and data analysis to real business challenges.

The team provided three Excel datasets obtained from a third-party industry data provider, each containing over 100,000 rows of historical and forecasted spirits market data. These datasets included sales volume and sales value across countries, product categories, and time periods.

I took the initiative to explore the data and develop a Python-based analytical workflow, using large language models (LLMs) as development assistance. I used the resulting analysis to investigate industry trends, compare market performance, and create visualizations that could help communicate meaningful insights to business leaders.

The final deliverable was a presentation summarizing key findings, market trends, and potential strategic considerations.

## Project Objectives

The primary goal was to transform large, complex industry datasets into understandable and actionable business insights.

The analysis focused on answering several questions:

- Is the spirits industry growing through increased sales volume or higher sales value per case?
- How do different spirits categories compare in historical performance and projected growth?
- Which geographic markets demonstrate strong growth potential?
- How have industry trends changed since 2019, before the COVID-19 pandemic?
- What do industry forecasts through 2030 suggest about future opportunities and challenges?

## Data Analysis & Methodology

### 1. Data Processing and Preparation

The project began with three large Excel datasets containing historical and projected market information.

Using Python and Pandas, the analysis involved:

- Importing and processing large Excel datasets.
- Selecting relevant countries, categories, metrics, and time periods.
- Filtering and organizing data for comparisons.
- Preparing structured datasets for calculations and visualizations.

### 2. Market Performance Analysis

The analysis compared sales volume and sales value across multiple dimensions of the spirits industry.

Key analytical methods included:

- **Year-over-Year Growth:** Evaluating annual changes in market performance.
- **Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR):** Comparing growth across multi-year periods.
- **Sales Volume vs. Sales Value:** Distinguishing changes in consumption from changes in market value.
- **Market Share Analysis:** Comparing the relative size and performance of spirits categories.
- **Geographic Comparisons:** Examining differences in historical and projected performance across countries.
- **Historical vs. Forecast Analysis:** Comparing past trends with industry projections through 2030.

Forecast figures were supplied in the original datasets and analyzed as part of the project; the project did not involve developing the underlying forecasting models.

### 3. Data Visualization

Python-generated visualizations were used to communicate findings that would be difficult to interpret from raw data alone.

Visualizations emphasized:

- Historical industry performance and future projections.
- Comparisons between sales value and volume.
- Growth differences across product categories and geographic markets.
- Pre-pandemic benchmarks and forecasted market trends.
- Changes in market share and category performance.

These visualizations were incorporated into a PowerPoint presentation designed to communicate the analysis to a business audience.

## Key Findings & Business Insights

### 1. Industry Shift Toward Value-Based Growth

One of the central findings was that spirits industry sales volume was projected to decline while overall sales value showed modest growth.

This suggested that higher-value products and premiumization could play an increasingly important role in industry performance, even when total quantities sold decrease.

**Business implication:** Focusing on value per case and product positioning may offer opportunities beyond simply increasing sales volume.

### 2. Relative Stability of Whiskey Categories

Comparisons across spirits categories highlighted American and Irish whiskey as relatively stable segments within the company's portfolio.

The analysis identified the potential importance of these categories in a changing market environment, including their relevance to the Sagamore Spirit and The Busker brands.

**Business implication:** Established whiskey brands may offer relative stability amid broader shifts in industry consumption.

### 3. Opportunities in Tequila

Category comparisons also identified tequila as a potential area for further market exploration.

**Business implication:** The category warranted additional consideration as a possible growth opportunity, subject to further market and financial evaluation.

### 4. Geographic and Long-Term Trends

Historical and projected data were analyzed across geographic markets to compare growth, market size, and value per case.

The analysis used 2019 as a pre-pandemic benchmark and incorporated estimates and forecasts extending through 2030.

**Business implication:** Understanding differences between countries and categories can help inform discussions around market prioritization and long-term industry positioning.

## Data Confidentiality & Limitations

The original datasets were obtained through Disaronno International USA from a third-party industry data provider and are not publicly distributed in this repository.

To respect the confidentiality of the underlying industry data:

- Original Excel datasets are excluded.
- Specific proprietary sales figures are not disclosed in this documentation.
- The presentation is shared only in a limited form with sensitive information obscured.
- The repository focuses on demonstrating the analytical approach, Python implementation, and high-level findings.

**Reproducibility:** The original analysis requires access to the proprietary source datasets. Consequently, the Python code cannot reproduce the original results without the corresponding data files.
